# 💖 Romantic Confession Website ("No Option To Reject")

A sweet, romantic, and playful confession website inspired by the viral **"She will have no option to reject your proposal"** coding trend!

---

## ✨ Features

- 💌 **Stage 1: Interactive 3D Envelope**: A sealed letter that opens when she taps it, revealing a special delivery just for her.
- 🌸 **Stage 2: Heartwarming Compliments Slider**: Shows 3 sweet memories / reasons why you like her before the big question.
- 🏃‍♂️ **Stage 3: The Viral Runaway "No" Button**:
  - The "No" button playfully dodges the mouse cursor or touch taps, making it impossible to reject!
  - As she tries to click "No", the "YES" button grows bigger and bigger.
  - Features 12 hilarious and cute pleading messages ("*Are you sure? 🥺*", "*Pookie please... 👉👈*", "*Error 404: 'No' not found!*").
  - Works smoothly on both **Desktop** and **Mobile**!
- 🎉 **Stage 4: Grand Celebration & Love Letter**:
  - Colorful confetti explosion and floating celebration hearts.
  - Heartfelt customizable love letter.
  - **WhatsApp Direct Button**: Allows her to send you an instant confirmation on WhatsApp with one click!
- 🎵 **Built-in Romantic Music Box Synth**:
  - Plays a gentle, nostalgic music box lullaby using the browser's Web Audio API.
  - **Zero external audio files needed** — plays instantly without lag or broken links!
- ⚙️ **Instant Customizer**:
  - Click the **⚙️ button** to enter her name, your name, custom reasons, your love letter, and your WhatsApp number.
  - Generates an **instant shareable URL** (e.g. `index.html#name=Sarah&sender=David...`) so you can send a personalized version directly to her without touching a line of code!
  - 4 Beautiful romantic themes: **Rose Blossom**, **Lavender Dream**, **Sunset Peach**, and **Midnight Sparkle**.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You don't need to install anything! Simply:

1. Double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
2. (Optional) Or run a local server:
   ```bash
   npx serve .
   ```

## 🌐 How to Host Online for FREE (To Send Her the Link)

To share this with your crush on WhatsApp or Instagram, host it online for free using any of these easy methods:

### Option A: GitHub Pages (Recommended)
1. Create a free repository on [GitHub](https://github.com).
2. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`.
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > select `main` branch and click **Save**.
4. GitHub will give you a live link like `https://yourusername.github.io/confession/`!

### Option B: Netlify Drop (Takes 30 seconds, No account needed)
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop this folder (`confessing`).
3. You will immediately get a live public link to share with her!

## 💌 How to Personalize Your Link

Once your site is live, you can personalize it in two ways:
1. Open your live website, tap the **⚙️ (Settings)** icon in the top-right corner.
2. Fill in:
   - **Her Name**
   - **Your Name**
   - **Your Proposal Question**
   - **Your WhatsApp Number** (with country code, e.g. `+1...` or `91...`)
   - **3 Reasons Why You Love Her**
   - **Your Personal Love Letter**
   - **Theme**
3. Click **"Copy"** next to the Shareable Link.
4. Send that copied link to her! When she opens it, everything will already be tailored just for her.
